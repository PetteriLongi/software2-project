Whispers of Guidance

The Premise

You are a traveler caught between worlds as reality itself unravels. Ancient conduits have opened across the globe, bleeding eldritch horrors into our realm. Armed with forbidden knowledge and your wits, you must traverse airports, close these rifts, and survive encounters with beings from beyond.

But beware—the Doom Clock is ticking. Every choice, every journey brings the end closer. Can you seal all five conduits before reality collapses entirely?

Controls

Story Mode:
  • SPACE or CLICK - Continue/Skip typing
  • CLICK choices to progress
Combat:
  • Attack - Strike with equipped weapon
  • Defend - Brace to halve incoming damage
  • Manifest - Use manifestation powers (costs sanity)
  • Reload - Reload firearms
Travel:
  • CLICK airport pins on map to view destinations
  • Travel costs Markka (currency) and advances Doom Clock
  • Rest at airports to restore HP/Sanity
Inventory:
  • Manage weapons, items, and manifestations
  • Use consumables to restore HP/Sanity
  • Equip better weapons as you find them

Key Concepts

  • Doom Clock: Tracks reality's collapse. Reaches zero = game over.
  • Conduits: 5 dimensional rifts you must close to win.
  • Manifestations: Eldritch powers unlocked through story choices.
  • Sanity: Used for manifestations. Losing all has consequences.
  • Markka: Finnish currency for travel and items.
